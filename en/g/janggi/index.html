Janggi

1–2 players · vs CPU or one phonePlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Checkmate your opponent's general to win! Korean traditional chess: Cho (blue) and Han (red) battle to protect their generals. Choose your Horse/Elephant setup and play the CPU on three levels or two players on one device. Win to climb from 18 kyu up to dan!

How to play

  1. Tap a piece to see where it can go → tap a spot to move
  2. Win by trapping the opposing General with no escape (checkmate)
  3. Chariot: straight to the end · Cannon: jumps over exactly one piece (can't jump or capture another Cannon) · Horse: one step straight then diagonal · Elephant: one step straight then two diagonal
  4. General and Guards stay inside the palace · Soldiers move one step forward or sideways · you can also move along the diagonal lines in the palace
  5. If the two generals face each other with nothing between, it's Bikjang — decided on points (Han gets a 1.5 bonus)
  6. There are Undo and Pass buttons · winning raises your rank points
