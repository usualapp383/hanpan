Ddakji Flip

1–2 players · online 1v1Play in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Flip your opponent's ddakji over to win! Tap the moving reticle to pick your spot, then tap the power gauge — slam! Just like real ddakji, the center won't budge; hit hard near the edge to flip it. Beat 8 rivals from the Little Kid to the Legendary Ddakji King and collect the ddakji you win.

How to play

  1. Tap once to stop the reticle → tap again to set power (Space works too)
  2. The center never flips · Near the edge + the gold power zone flips best
  3. First to flip the other's ddakji wins · The winner takes that ddakji
  4. You play with the thickest ddakji you've won — thicker is harder to flip
