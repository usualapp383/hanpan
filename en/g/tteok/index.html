Rice Cake Sort

Solo or 2–6 playersOpen play: 3 minutesPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Mugwort, pink, gardenia… colorful rice cakes are all mixed up on the skewers. Tap a skewer to move its top cake to another, until each skewer holds one kind! You can only place on the same color or an empty skewer. Every board is solved by the computer first, so it's always solvable.

How to play

  1. Tap a skewer to lift its top cake (same colors come along) · then tap where it goes
  2. You can only place on the same color or an empty skewer · up to 4 per skewer
  3. Each skewer with 4 of one kind = done!
  4. Stuck? ↶ Undo · 💡 Hint · ＋ Empty skewer (once per board)
  5. Free mode: how many boards in 3 minutes · fewer moves score more
