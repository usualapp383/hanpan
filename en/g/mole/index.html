Mole Bonk

Solo or 2–6 players60 secondsPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Bonk the moles popping out of their holes with your squeaky hammer! Catch as many as you can in 60 seconds. Golden moles are worth big points, helmet moles take two hits, and never hit the hedgehog.

How to play

  1. Tap the moles that pop out
  2. Consecutive hits build a combo · Combo 15 = Fever Time (2× points)
  3. Golden mole +50 · helmet mole takes two hits · 💣 bomb mole takes out every mole that is up
  4. Hedgehog −30 · clock mole +5s · the last 10 seconds are a rush
