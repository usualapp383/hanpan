Tuho Arrow Toss

1–2 players · one phone or onlinePlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Toss arrows into the pot for a high score! Tuho, the arrow-toss game of Joseon palaces. The mouth scores 10, and landing in a small side ear ("gwani") scores 20. Each round the pot gets farther and the wind picks up. Two players can take turns.

How to play

  1. Press, pull down and release to throw · pull more to throw farther
  2. Pull sideways to send it the other way · check the wind flag and aim off
  3. Mouth 10 pts · each ear 20 pts · streaks score bigger
  4. Five rounds · 5 arrows each · land all five for a Perfect Round bonus
  5. Keyboard: ←→ aim · hold Space, release to throw
