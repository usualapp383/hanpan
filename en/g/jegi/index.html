Jegi Kick

Solo or 2–6 playersPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Kick the jegi as many times as you can without letting it touch the ground! Tap under the falling jegi to kick it; kick it close to the foot for a perfect hit, and wind makes it sway. New records unlock new jegi.

How to play

  1. Tap where the jegi is falling to run over and kick
  2. Kick it close to the foot for a perfect hit! It flies straight up
  3. Game over if it hits the ground · Wind starts at 15
  4. Every 25 kicks a ✨ golden jegi: counts double for 5 kicks
