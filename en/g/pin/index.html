Retro Pinball

Solo or 2–6 playersPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Score as high as you can with 3 balls! Pinball with three tables to unlock: score 150K on the practice table, Starlight Park, and the top-right hole becomes a gate to the next table (Deep Sea score ×2 · Dragon Keep ×3). Guard the ball with 4 flippers and aim for bumpers, targets and the hole. Light all 3 top stars for multiball with two balls at once; drop all 4 targets to raise the multiplier.

How to play

  1. Left side = both left flippers, right side = right flipper (← → or Z · /)
  2. When the ball is on the launcher, press and hold, then release to launch (Space)
  3. Light 3 top stars → Multiball! · 4 targets → Multiplier UP · Upper-right hole +2000
  4. Pink zigzag walls are springs — boing! · The middle bumper slides left and right
  5. When the KICK light between the flippers is on, a draining ball gets shot back up once (relights after knocking down all 4 right targets twice)
  6. For the first few seconds, you get the ball back if it drains
  7. At 150K the top-right hole becomes a vortex gate! Enter it for Deep Sea (score ×2, +1 ball) · at 800K, Dragon Keep (×3)
