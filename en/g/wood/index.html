Carpenter's Line

Solo or 2–6 playersPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Look at the top piece and draw the cut line on the bottom board with your finger. Tap “Cut” to saw and test the fit; gaps show in red. Twelve joints — half-lap, dovetail, half-circle lock, finger, sawtooth, wedge… — plus “Which line is right?” quizzes. Learn the basics of hand joinery for fun.

How to play

  1. Trace the bottom edge of the top piece onto the lower board, left edge to right edge in one stroke
  2. Tap “Cut” to saw → the top piece drops in · gaps show in red
  3. The first few rounds (6 on Easy, 3 otherwise) show a faint guide line · after that, draw by eye
  4. Quiz rounds: tap the right line of three · an explanation follows
  5. Drew it wrong? Tap “Redraw”
