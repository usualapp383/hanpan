One-Stroke Hanbok

SoloPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Draw every line in one stroke without tracing any line twice! At the last stage of each chapter, draw a hanbok piece such as a jeogori, skirt or flower shoes in one stroke and the lady wears it. Collect stars to unlock new hanbok colors.

How to play

  1. Start from a dot and drag along the lines to paint with the brush
  2. You can't trace the same line twice · Draw every line to win
  3. Go back to undo one step · Stuck? ↺ Restart
  4. No undos = 3 stars · Using a hint = 1 star
