Janggu Beat

Solo or 2–6 playersPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Drum along to the flowing beat and make it to the end before the excitement fades! Gutgeori · Semachi · Jajinmori · Hwimori: tap left (gung) and right (deok), and 'deong' is both hands at once. Hit it clean and the "Eolssu!" cheers grow; miss and the mood cools. Learn Korean traditional rhythms with your hands.

How to play

  1. When a marker reaches the line, tap left for kung, right for deok (keys F · J)
  2. 'Deong' is both hands at once (or Space)
  3. Perfect · good · miss — combos build, full mood gauge doubles points
  4. Game over when the mood hits zero · speeds up every 4 bars
