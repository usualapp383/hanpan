Buddy Jump Rope

1–2 players · one phonePlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Work together and cross the cliffs all the way to the end! Dodo and Toto are tied by one rope: left side is Dodo, right side is Toto. Play solo with two hands or with a friend on one phone. If your buddy falls, hold steady and pull them up.

How to play

  1. Left half = Dodo, right half = Toto. Hold to walk forward, tap to jump (tap while walking = long jump forward)
  2. Hold and swipe up to jump too · Drag left to walk backward. A friend hanging on the rope climbs up the rope while you hold
  3. Standing still, you hold firm. If you pull while walking, you may get dragged and fall!
  4. Jump together within 0.25 s for 💞 perfect sync +50 · From stage 3, 🌬 gusts push you in the air · If both fall, a ☁ cloud catches you once per stage
