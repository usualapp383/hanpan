Monster Survival

Solo · online co-opPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Survive as long as you can amid the swarming monsters! Just move — your weapons attack on their own. Pick 1 of 3 cards each level-up to build your combo, evolve weapons into gold, and spend collected gold on permanent upgrades.

How to play

  1. Press and drag anywhere on the screen to move that way (arrow keys·WASD work too)
  2. Pick a character and starting weapon in the lobby, and fill your codex
  3. Weapons attack on their own. Pick up gems enemies drop for XP
  4. Level up to pick one of 3 cards
  5. A 12s breather first at 75s, then every 90s · each boss attacks differently (dodge the red circles and lines)
  6. Reach each territory's kill (or level) target and its boss appears · defeat it to conquer and end the run
  7. Score = kill points + gems collected + seconds survived + conquest bonus
