Fruit Merge

Solo or 2–6 playersPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Merge matching fruits to grow bigger ones! Drop fruits so matching ones touch and they turn into a bigger fruit. From blueberry to watermelon! Cross the line above the box and it's over.

How to play

  1. Drag left/right to set the position and release to drop (← → · Space)
  2. Two matching fruits merge into the next fruit when they touch
  3. Make 5 big fruits to earn a 💣 bomb · tap the bomb slot and drop it to clear nearby fruit (B)
  4. Game over if fruit stays above the red line too long · Merge two watermelons for a big bonus
