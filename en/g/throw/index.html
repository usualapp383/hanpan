Pebble Duel

1–2 players · vs CPU, one phone or onlinePlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Hit your opponent with stones and knock them down first to win! Set your angle, hold to charge, and throw. Read the wind and turn it around with 4 power-ups! Two players can share one phone.

How to play

  1. Press the screen to build power, and while holding, set the angle with your finger position. Release to throw
  2. Watch the wind arrow. Headwind: hit harder; tailwind: go gently
  3. Chances (Double · Giant stone · Band-aid · Cat) are one each per round. Press before throwing
