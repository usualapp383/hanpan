Mini Soccer

1–2 players · vs CPU or one phone2-minute matchPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Score more goals in 2 minutes to win! Top-down 4v4 arcade soccer. Run with the stick, link up passes, and shoot! Tackle to steal when you don't have the ball. Golden goal if tied. Two players can share one device, too.

How to play

  1. Stick: Move (1P arrow keys · 2P WASD)
  2. With the ball: pass (S) · shoot (D) — kicks toward the stick · 2-player: 1P N·M / 2P G·F
  3. Without the ball: Tackle · Switch player · Auto-switch to the player nearest the ball
  4. Complete 3 passes in a row for a 💥 power shot (fast, hard for the keeper) · When trailing, the last 30 s is chase mode: your players run faster
