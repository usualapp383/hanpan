Penalty Shootout

1–2 players · online 1v1Play in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Take turns shooting and saving, and keep the streak going as long as you can without a single miss! Aim for every corner of the goal, and dive to stop incoming shots. The keeper and kicker get tougher as you go.

How to play

  1. Shooting: tap the goal to aim (top left · bottom right …) → hold below and release in the green
  2. Corners are harder to save but easier to miss · Red on the gauge = over the bar
  3. To save: tap exactly where the ball is flying · The keeper's hands go there · Tap too early and you dive the wrong way
