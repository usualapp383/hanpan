Sudoku

SoloPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Fill the blanks so each row, column and bold 3×3 box has 1 to 9 exactly once. Notes and hints available; 3 mistakes and it's over. Solve one and the next starts right away!

How to play

  1. Tap a blank, then tap a number below (PC: number & arrow keys)
  2. Wrong numbers turn red and count as 1 mistake
  3. Use notes mode to jot down small candidate numbers
  4. 3 hints per puzzle · faster solves earn a bonus
