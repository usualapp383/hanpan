Fruit Slash

Solo or 2–6 playersPlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Swipe to slice the flying fruit! Slice several at once for a combo, and never touch the bombs.

How to play

  1. Swipe fast and the blade slices the fruit (mouse drag works too)
  2. Slice 3+ in one swipe for a combo bonus
  3. 10 · 25 · 50 slices without a miss = blade frenzy (score ×2) · 🍉 giant melon needs two cuts for +10 · 🛡 shield fruit blocks one bomb
  4. Classic: Miss 3 fruits or slice a bomb and it's over
  5. 60s: As many as you can! Bombs are -10
  6. Blue ice fruit slows time, golden star fruit brings a Fruit Storm!
