Gonggi Jacks

1–2 players · one phone or onlinePlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

Pick up and catch the stones to rack up points ("nyeon")! With two players, the first to 10 points wins. Toss one stone up, grab stones from the floor before it lands, and catch it. Clear stages 1 to 4, then in the finale every stone you catch on the back of your hand is a point.

How to play

  1. Tap the screen to toss the stone in your hand
  2. While it's in the air, tap stones on the floor to pick them · the number changes each stage
  3. Once you have them, tap anywhere as the stone comes down (green ring) to catch
  4. Stage 4: toss, tap once to drop four stones, catch → toss again and grab all four at once
  5. Finale: toss all five, catch them on the back of your hand (tap as each falls), then flip and snatch — each stone is a point
  6. A mistake costs a life solo, or passes the turn with two players
  7. Keyboard: Space to toss and catch, ← → to choose a stone, ↓ to pick it up
