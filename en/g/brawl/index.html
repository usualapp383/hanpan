Super Strike

1–2 players · vs CPU, one phone or onlinePlay in your browser, no install
▶ Play now

An arcade brawler: drain each foe's health first and beat them one by one! Pull off specials with commands like ↓↘→ + Punch! Hold back to block, or just tap the Special button to pull off moves.

How to play

  1. Joystick: move · up to jump · down to crouch · hold away from opponent to block (crouch + hold to block low)
  2. Mash punch · kick buttons for combos. Down+kick = leg sweep, up close forward+punch = grab & throw
  3. ↓↘→+Punch, →↓↘+Punch, ↓↙←+Kick command specials · Easy special: Special button (no direction/up/back) · Full gauge unleashes an ultimate
  4. Beat Shadow Typhoon to unlock endless challenge! Opponents keep getting stronger, and your record is how many you beat
